the everyday foundations

Good habits, with familiar foods.

Eat enough

Try dal, chana, rajma, eggs, fish or other protein with roti or rice and seasonal sabzi. Iron-rich foods matter when you bleed.

Move in ways that fit

Walking, dancing, household activity and simple strength exercises all count. Adjust for pain, energy and ability.

Protect recovery

Drink safe water, get regular sleep and allow time for recovery.

about the guidance

How we make recommendations.

Suggestions are based on common nutritional needs, menstrual symptoms, strength and bone health. Phase estimates are approximate. For contraception, pregnancy planning or persistent symptoms, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

For general guidance, see NHS menopause information, ACOG women's health and WHO physical activity guidance.
