I've moved between manufacturing, optics, thermal systems, plasma physics, product design and environmental engineering. On paper, that looks like a lot of different directions.

environmental engineering ENGINEERING / MASTER’S RESEARCH

Waste, systems,
and the missing capacity.

MTech Environmental Engineering · BITS Pilani · Research in progress

When a plant exists on paper, why can't it always do the job?

My master's research looks at the gap between installed municipal waste-treatment capacity and the waste that actually gets processed in India.

I look at what happens when a machine breaks, a repair drags on, or the waste coming in can’t actually be processed. Then I model when fixing the equipment helps — and when it doesn’t.

These are exploratory scenarios, not plant forecasts.

what i care about

Making room
for more people.

Good design should make life a little less difficult to navigate.

Accessibility is one of the themes I keep returning to. I've thought about haptic navigation glasses for blind and low-vision users, and ways to make movement inside homes without elevators less difficult for older adults.

These are early ideas, not tested assistive devices. And that distinction matters: useful accessibility design needs direct input from the people it's meant for, careful testing and attention to safety.

I want to keep learning how to build with people, rather than just designing for them from a distance.
