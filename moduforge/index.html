a short introduction · engineering orchestration concept

Engineering should be easier to access.

What if getting something made was as easy as explaining what you need?

ModuForge AI is a proposed engineering orchestration platform that helps people navigate custom physical solutions—from describing a problem to engineering review and fabrication.

explore the interactive concept ↗how it could work ↓
01 / the problem

A real need rarely comes with a drawing.

Custom physical solutions are difficult to navigate when you're not an engineer. Designers, engineers, suppliers and installers work separately. Cost, feasibility, safety and timelines are hard to understand.

“I know what I need, but not how to build it.”
02 / the approach

One journey. Coordinated end to end.

AI helps organize the work. Qualified people make the safety-critical decisions.

01 / describe

Tell us the problem.

Share what you need, plus photos of the space or object.

02 / explore

Explore options.

Structure requirements and find possible CAD and design routes.

03 / validate

Check it properly.

Qualified engineers assess fit, feasibility and safety.

04 / deliver

Make it real.

Coordinate transparent quotes, fabrication and installation.

03 / first proposed application · eldercare

Independence shouldn't end at the staircase.

StepHome explores tailored mobility solutions for older adults living in homes without elevators.

Stairs can limit daily movement, connection and independence. Every home and person needs careful assessment and safe engineering—not a one-size-fits-all answer.

04 / why i'm exploring this

I want engineering to be useful beyond the factory.

I've worked on taking physical products from design and validation into production. Eldercare feels like a practical place to start: tangible problems, personal impact and a real need for thoughtful design.

05 / what matters

Not AI replacing engineers.

Human-led safety

Qualified engineering review before safety-critical fabrication.

Transparent choices

Visible drawings, components, quotes and decisions.

Reusable knowledge

Open-source-minded design and CAD resources where appropriate.

06 / the conversation i'm looking for

Help me understand the real problem.

I'm looking to speak with older adults, caregivers and eldercare organizations about mobility challenges at home. The goal is to listen before building.
