an industrialization workflow.

This illustrates the activities that connect a design change to production. It is a workflow view, not a claim that every stage is solely my responsibility.

01 · Define the changeCapture requirements, current design and the manufacturing or cost constraint.
02 · Review feasibilityStudy DFM, tolerances, interfaces, process limitations and design risks.
03 · Develop the supply routeIdentify capable suppliers and toolmakers; review tooling, materials and drawings.
04 · ValidateCoordinate verification, assembly checks, quality evidence and change approvals.
05 · TransitionManage documentation, manufacturing readiness, inventory considerations and production changeover.
06 · Verify the outcomeDistinguish design savings, tariff avoidance, production costs and actual operating results.
Why the distinctions matter: a part that passes drawing review is not necessarily ready for production; and a gross cost avoidance figure is not automatically a net saving.
